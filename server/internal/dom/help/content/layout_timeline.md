## Timeline layout

The timeline shows each task as a row: its status, title, assignee and #id on the left, and a bar from its start date to its due date on a day axis on the right. A line marks today.

**Best for:** seeing how the work lines up over the coming weeks, and who or what it belongs to.

**How a task shows:**
- With a **start date** and a **due date**: a bar across those days.
- With a **due date** only: a dot on that day.
- With a **start date** and no due date: a dot with a tail, open-ended.
- A task past its due date and not closed is outlined in red; a closed one is dimmed.

**Zoom and scroll:** **Week**, **Month** and **Quarter** set how much fits across. Scroll sideways with Shift + the wheel, by dragging the empty axis, or with **‹ ›**; **Today** brings today back into view. The zoom and where you are stay in the link.

**Bands:** the filter bar's GROUP (under the Display menu) splits the rows into bands — by milestone, assignee, component, status and so on. Bands follow their own order: milestones by their date (shown as ◆ on the band's row), statuses in workflow order, `(unset)` last. Click a band to fold it.

**Moving dates:**
- Drag a bar to move it: both dates move by the same number of days.
- Drag either end of a bar to change just that date. An end can't pass the other end.
- **Unscheduled** (top right) lists the filtered tasks with neither date. Drag one onto the axis to give it that day as its due date.
- A tooltip names where it would land; **Esc** cancels. A start date can't be after the due date. kitp refuses such a change, and the bar goes back.

**Keyboard:** **↑ ↓** (or **j k**) move between rows, **Enter** opens the task (or folds a band), **Alt + ← →** moves the task a day earlier or later (**Alt + Shift**: a week) — the same change as dragging — and **t** scrolls to today.
