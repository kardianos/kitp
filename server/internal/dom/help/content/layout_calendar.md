## Calendar layout

The calendar shows the screen's tasks by date: a month of six weeks, or one week (**Month** | **Week**). Use **‹ ›** to step a month (a week) and **Today** to come back; the month you're on stays in the link, so a reload or a shared link opens the same one.

**Best for:** seeing what's due when, and moving dates around.

**How a task shows:**
- With a **due date** only, it sits on that day.
- With a **start date** and a **due date**, it's a bar across those days, wrapping at the week's end (a bar that goes on into the next week is marked "…").
- With a **start date** and no due date, it sits on its start day, marked →.
- A **milestone** with a date shows as ◆ by its day's number.
- A task past its due date and not closed is outlined in red; a closed one is dimmed.
- When a day has more than fits, **+N more** opens the day's full list.

**Moving dates:**
- Drag a task to another day: both its dates move by the same number of days, so its length stays the same. The days it would land on are shaded as you drag, and a tooltip names them. **Esc** cancels.
- **Unscheduled** (top right) lists the filtered tasks with neither date. Drag one onto a day to give it that due date.
- A start date can't be after the due date. kitp refuses such a change, and the task goes back to where it was.

**The filter bar:** search, **+ Filter**, the phase toggles and saved views work as on every screen. The calendar doesn't group, so GROUP isn't offered here.

**Keyboard:** click a day (or Tab into the calendar), then **← →** move a day, **↑ ↓** a week, **PageUp / PageDown** a month (a week), **Enter** opens the day's first task and **t** goes to today.
